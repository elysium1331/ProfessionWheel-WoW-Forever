# Image request: ProfessionWheel profession icons, batch 1

Generate 13 original icons with your image generation tool and save each as a PNG at
`art/icons/raw/<key>.png`, relative to the current working directory. Create the
folder if needed. Change no other files.

## Where they are used

Each icon sits inside one round slot of a radial wheel: a dark near-black well
(#17140f) framed by a hammered-gold ring. Shown at **40-80 px**. The attached image
(`art/curseforge/logo.png`) is the style reference: **match its five painted
symbols exactly in style** (spool with needle, hammer over anvil, potion flask,
pickaxe, fishing hook): painterly but clean fantasy, warm ivory / steel / wood /
bronze materials with one saturated accent, soft top-left light, bold readable
silhouette.

## Rules (all 13)

- **Background: flat pure magenta #FF00FF** everywhere outside the symbol; no
  shadow, glow, gradient or texture on it, and no magenta inside the symbol (it is
  keyed out automatically).
- **No disc, circle, ring, frame, plate or badge** behind the symbol: just the
  symbol. The wheel draws the dark well and gold ring itself.
- The symbol is centered and fits inside a circle covering about 78% of the canvas.
- Square, 1024 × 1024. One symbol per icon, simple enough to read at 40 px.
- Same scale, lighting and visual weight across all 13 so they look like one set,
  and like the five symbols in the reference.
- No text, letters, numbers or runes that look like letters. Original artwork only:
  do not imitate World of Warcraft / Blizzard icons.
