# Image request: ProfessionWheel CurseForge art, batch 1

Generate 3 original images using your image generation tool and save each as a PNG
at `art/curseforge/raw/<file>`, relative to the current working directory. Create
the folder if it doesn't exist. Change no other files.

## What the addon is

[Elysium] ProfessionWheel is a World of Warcraft addon. One key opens a round
radial wheel around the mouse cursor. The wheel shows the player's current
professions (up to 5: e.g. tailoring, enchanting, cooking, fishing, first aid,
mining, alchemy), and clicking one opens that profession. The in-game wheel is a
dark near-black disc (#17140f) with a muted-gold rim (#9e8552), round slots with
thin gold rings, and a small dark hub in the center.

## Style (all 3 images)

- Restrained fantasy, painterly but clean. Bold shapes that still read when the
  image is shown small (the logo appears as small as 64 px on CurseForge).
- Palette: near-black and deep warm brown, muted and bright gold, warm ivory and
  parchment, with small saturated accents (crimson, teal, amber).
- Soft light from the top-left, a subtle warm glow from the wheel's hub.
- Original artwork only. Do not imitate or reference World of Warcraft icons,
  Blizzard art, the WoW logo, faction crests, or any existing addon's logo.
- No text, letters, numbers, words or watermarks in any image.
